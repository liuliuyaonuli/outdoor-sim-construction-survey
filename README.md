# Outdoor Simulation Environment Construction Survey

Companion repository for **Automated Construction of Outdoor 3D Simulation Environments for Autonomous Systems**.

**中文题目：面向自主系统的室外三维仿真环境自动构建**

## Overview

This survey examines the automated construction of outdoor 3D simulation environments for autonomous systems, including autonomous vehicles and unmanned aerial vehicles. It connects environment construction methods with simulation requirements, consistency maintenance, evaluation, and method selection.

The literature is organized around three construction tasks:

| Task | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Synthesis of new environments | Establish new environmental content and spatial structure through procedural rules, asset composition, and learned priors. |
| Reconstruction of real environments | Recover a specific real environment from maps and sensor observations, distinguishing observation-based recovery from prior-based completion. |
| Transformation of existing environments | Modify geometry, objects, or attributes while preserving the environmental content that must be retained. |

Hybrid construction and automated orchestration connect these tasks into broader workflows.

## Review outline

1. Introduction
2. Scope, terminology, and review method
3. A task-centered taxonomy of environment construction
4. Simulation use and consistency maintenance of construction outputs
5. Evaluation and method selection
6. Open questions and future directions
7. Conclusion
