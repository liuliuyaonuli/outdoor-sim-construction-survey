# 📘 Supporting Literature

Technical background, resource references, and surveys used in Chapter 3.

## Technical Background

- **Structure-from-Motion Revisited**\
  <sub>CVPR 2016</sub> · [📄 Paper](https://demuc.de/papers/schoenberger2016sfm.pdf)

Foundational camera registration and sparse structure recovery.

- **Pixelwise View Selection for Unstructured Multi-View Stereo**\
  <sub>ECCV 2016</sub> · [📄 Paper](https://demuc.de/papers/schoenberger2016mvs.pdf)

Foundational view selection, depth, and normal estimation for dense reconstruction.

- **SuGaR: Surface-Aligned Gaussian Splatting for Efficient 3D Mesh Reconstruction and High-Quality Mesh Rendering**\
  <sub>CVPR 2024</sub> · [📄 Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Guedon_SuGaR_Surface-Aligned_Gaussian_Splatting_for_Efficient_3D_Mesh_Reconstruction_and_CVPR_2024_paper.html)

General surface-aligned Gaussian and mesh-recovery technique.

- **2D Gaussian Splatting for Geometrically Accurate Radiance Fields**\
  <sub>ACM SIGGRAPH Conference Papers 2024</sub> · [📄 Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3641519.3657428)

General planar-Gaussian surface representation and geometric regularization.

- **Instruct-NeRF2NeRF: Editing 3D Scenes with Instructions**\
  <sub>ICCV 2023</sub> · [📄 Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Haque_Instruct-NeRF2NeRF_Editing_3D_Scenes_with_Instructions_ICCV_2023_paper.html)

General instruction-driven NeRF editing mechanism used as technical background.

- **GaussianEditor: Swift and Controllable 3D Editing with Gaussian Splatting**\
  <sub>CVPR 2024</sub> · [📄 Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Chen_GaussianEditor_Swift_and_Controllable_3D_Editing_with_Gaussian_Splatting_CVPR_2024_paper.html)

General semantic target tracking and Gaussian editing mechanism used as technical background.


## Map and Asset References

- **Objaverse: A Universe of Annotated 3D Objects**\
  <sub>CVPR 2023</sub> · [📄 Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Deitke_Objaverse_A_Universe_of_Annotated_3D_Objects_CVPR_2023_paper.html)

Reusable annotated 3D object collection; listed as an asset resource.

- **GenAssets: Generating in-the-wild 3D Assets in Latent Space**\
  <sub>CVPR 2025</sub> · [📄 Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Yang_GenAssets_Generating_in-the-wild_3D_Assets_in_Latent_Space_CVPR_2025_paper.html)

Foreground-asset generation and replacement; listed under assets rather than counted as an environment-construction pipeline.

- **OpenStreetMap: User-Generated Street Maps**\
  <sub>IEEE Pervasive Computing 2008</sub> · [📄 Paper](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/13849/)

Geographic map source used by construction pipelines.
